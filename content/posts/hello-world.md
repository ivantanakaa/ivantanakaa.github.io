Welcome to Tanaka's Table! 🍜

This is my first post, and I'm glad you found your way here.

## What this blog is about

Food has a way of bringing people together. A perfectly seasoned bowl of ramen on a cold evening, a slice of cake shared at a celebration, the smell of fresh bread in the morning — these small moments add up to something meaningful.

This blog is my space to explore that. Expect:

- **Restaurant visits** — honest takes, no sponsorships
- **Home cooking experiments** — the successes and the failures
- **Food culture** — why we eat what we eat, and what it means

## A little about me

I'm Tanaka. I believe a good meal should make you smile. Not just because the food tastes good, but because of the care that went into it, the company you share it with, and the memories it creates.
