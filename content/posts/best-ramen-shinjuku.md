It was raining. My feet hurt. I had already walked past three ramen shops with lines that spilled onto the sidewalk.

Then I found it — a place with no English sign, six seats, and a cook who looked like he'd been making the same broth for thirty years.

## The bowl

**Tonkotsu shoyu**, deep amber, with a layer of fragrant chicken oil floating on top. The chashu was practically falling apart. The noodles — thin, straight, with just enough chew — disappeared faster than I would like to admit.

I asked for a second serving of noodles (*kaedama*). The cook nodded without looking up.

## Practical info

- **Area:** Shinjuku, a few minutes east of the station
- **Price:** Around ¥1,100 for a bowl
- **Wait:** 20–30 min on weeknights, more on weekends
- **Hours:** 6pm – 2am (closed Tuesdays)
- **Best for:** Late nights when you need something serious
